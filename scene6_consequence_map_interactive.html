Cryptographically Relevant Quantum Computer
THE CRQC
WHAT IT IS
A CRQC is a quantum computer with enough stable, error-corrected logical qubits to run Shor's algorithm at scale — breaking RSA and ECC in hours, not billions of years.
TIMELINE ESTIMATES
Most intelligence assessments place a CRQC between 2030 and 2040. CISA, NSA, and NIST all treat this as a certainty — the debate is only when, not if.
WHY IT MATTERS NOW
Harvest Now, Decrypt Later: adversaries are already collecting encrypted traffic today to decrypt retroactively once a CRQC exists. The attack starts before the computer does.

Consequence #1
QUANTUM MAN-IN-THE-MIDDLE
HOW IT WORKS
Every TLS handshake, VPN tunnel, and SSH session uses RSA or ECC for key exchange. A CRQC breaks those key exchanges — making all encrypted traffic readable, past and present.
AFFECTED SYSTEMS
HTTPS / TLS 1.3 — all web traffic
VPN tunnels — IPSec, WireGuard, OpenVPN
SSH sessions — server access, git, CI/CD
Signal, WhatsApp, iMessage
Corporate email encryption (S/MIME)
RETROACTIVE RISK
Nation-states and sophisticated adversaries are storing intercepted traffic today. Medical records, legal communications, state secrets from 2024 become readable in 2034.
IMPACT: ALL ENCRYPTED INTERNET TRAFFIC

Consequence #2
PKI COLLAPSE
THE ROOT OF TRUST BREAKS
Certificate Authorities sign certificates using RSA or ECDSA. A CRQC can forge any certificate for any domain — making the CA-signed trust model that underpins HTTPS completely meaningless.
AFFECTED SYSTEMS
Browser HTTPS padlock — forgeable for any site
Code signing — malware becomes indistinguishable
Document signing — contracts, PDFs, legal records
DNSSEC — DNS trust chain breaks
Email authenticity (DKIM signatures)
WHY IT'S SYSTEMIC
PKI is the trust layer everything else sits on. Banking, government portals, software updates — all depend on certificate integrity. When PKI fails, nothing can be authenticated.
IMPACT: ALL DIGITAL TRUST INFRASTRUCTURE

Consequence #3
ICS / SCADA EXPOSURE
THE PHYSICAL WORLD AT RISK
Industrial Control Systems managing power grids, water treatment, oil pipelines, and manufacturing were built for decades of operation — using crypto that can't be patched quickly.
AFFECTED INFRASTRUCTURE
Electrical grid control systems (SCADA)
Water treatment and distribution
Oil and gas pipeline monitoring
Nuclear facility control networks
Hospital and medical device networks
THE UPGRADE PROBLEM
ICS devices have 20–30 year lifespans. Cryptographic updates require physical replacement or extended downtime — timelines measured in years, not months. Migration must start now.
